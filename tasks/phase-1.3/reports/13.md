# p1.3-13 — The rotation probe

One silence is reported as the refusal it is. A job handed the **new** candidate
of an `unverified` credential rotation whose first login is silent now sends
**one blank login** - this board's own key and no password, the `acl` login every
status read makes - before its second try. Answered at any level, the repeater
is on the air, and a second silence to the candidate right after it is the
password's silence: the job ends `unauthorized` with
`{"reason":"silentAfterReachable","probeAnsweredAs":"<tier>"}` as its data, which
is the contract's `LoginSilentResult` byte for byte, and meshmaster.dk's
fallback to the old candidate (CONFIGURATION §7 step 4) finally works for an
onboard exit node. A probe that is silent too proves nothing and the job is
`unreachable` as before; the legacy `LOGIN_FAIL` frame to the probe is an answer
and says `refused`. One transaction more, on that path only; no session stored
by it; an `old` candidate, or no candidate, never probes.

```
[14:02:11] I jobs: job 2231 config.read for repeater 17 "Risskov Water Tower" (admin)
[14:02:19] I jobs: job 2231: reachability probe (blank login): answered at read-only; the repeater is on the air
[14:02:27] W jobs: job 2231: the repeater answered a blank login at read-only and then said nothing to the new candidate; reported unauthorized (silentAfterReachable)
[14:02:27] I jobs: job 2231: unauthorized after 3 transaction(s)
```

The policy is the .NET agent's `EnsureSessionAsync` / `ProbeReachableAsync`
(task p4.7-95), matched decision for decision: the probe runs only when the
material is the `new` candidate and not blank, only after the **first** try was
silent, costs one transaction through the gate, stores no session, and the
**second** try is the one judged. It was written as a host-portable sequence
(`src/agent/LoginSequence.h/.cpp`) that `JobRunner::ensureSession` drives
through a `LoginOnTheAir` exchange - the division `ConfigApply.h` and
`ApplyOnTheAir` already make for an apply - so that every path runs on a host
against a scripted repeater, which is where the whole of the evidence is until
a board meets a real one.

## Deviations from the spec or the task

- **The tests are a new suite, `test/test_rotation_probe`, not `test_claim`.**
  The task says "`test_claim` (or wherever `ensureSession`'s outcomes are tested
  against the fake mesh)", and there is no such place: `JobRunner.cpp` is not in
  `[env:native]`'s `build_src_filter` (it needs Arduino, the radio and the RTC),
  `test_claim` tests the claim envelope, and the fake repeater is task p1.1-08's
  host harness, still stopped on its branch (`tasks/phase-1.1/logs/STOPPED-08.md`).
  So the sequence was lifted out of `ensureSession` into
  `src/agent/LoginSequence.h/.cpp` - the loop, the probe's place in it, and the
  verdict - behind a three-method `LoginExchange` that `JobRunner::LoginOnTheAir`
  implements over `loginOnce` and `probeReachable`, and the suite walks it against
  a scripted repeater that counts logins, probes, transactions and sessions. The
  board's half is held on `JobRunner.cpp`'s text the way `test_config_apply`
  holds the writes. `JobRunner::LoginResult` is now an alias of `LoginTry` so
  that nothing else in the runner moved. 95's finding lists six host tests
  "mirroring `RotationProbeTests`"; all six are here, and the one it words as "a
  pair with no entry on a repeater with a guest password → the probe silent" is
  on a host the same scripted silence as a repeater off the air, which is the
  point of EXIT-NODES §7's `acl` note.
- **The docs paragraph is in `docs/console.md`, not `docs/ops/agent.md`.** This
  repository has no `docs/ops/`; the login lines of a job are described in
  `docs/console.md` under the job log, and that is where the paragraph went,
  pointing at CONFIGURATION §7 step 4. The bench walk's item 17 (the rotation)
  gained the lost-reply case.
- **`ProbeAnswer` has no "not sent".** The task's `probeReachable` returns
  `false` both when the gate refused the send and when the blank login was
  silent, and the sequence treats the two alike (nothing learned, and the second
  try then meets the same lease and ends `timeout`), so the exchange's answer
  has three values, not four; the job log says which it was.
- **No disagreement between the spec at today's `master` of meshmaster.dk
  (`595d0f3`) and the task file.** CONFIGURATION §7, EXIT-NODES §7, DECISIONS A-4,
  `Handlers.cs`, `Results.cs` and 95's report were read there in full;
  `handleLoginReq` and `onContactResponse` were read on disk at `d9296435`
  (`examples/simple_repeater/MyMesh.cpp:89-141`,
  `examples/companion_radio/MyMesh.cpp:676-710`), and they say what A-4 says.

## Decisions taken without asking

- **Three serial lines for the probe.** The job log goes to the server; the
  runbook asks that the console print enough for the bench walk to be a matter
  of reading, so `probeReachable` adds one `LOG_I` per answer (`reachability
  probe (blank login): answered at <tier>; the repeater is on the air`, `…
  refused; …`, `… no reply; nothing is known about the password`) and
  `ensureSession` one `LOG_W` for the verdict (`reported unauthorized
  (silentAfterReachable)`). The job log's own lines are the .NET agent's
  `log.Info` / `log.Warn` texts verbatim, so the job's page reads the same on
  either agent.
- **The probe does not set the job's RTT.** `loginOnce` records the first
  answered round trip as `rttMs`; the probe leaves it alone, so a job whose only
  answered exchange was the probe reports no `rttMs` - the number is for the
  job's own exchanges, and the .NET agent's probe measures nothing either.
- **A probe answered above the grant sends no restore.** `restoreAcl` acts on
  the session in force, and the probe stores none; on stock firmware a blank
  login does not rewrite the entry (`handleLoginReq`'s first statement is a
  lookup), so there is nothing the probe changed to put back, and an entry left
  at admin by an earlier session that died before its restore is put right by
  the next job's own login, as the third rule of EXIT-NODES §7 already says. The
  .NET agent's probe stores nothing either.
- **The gate's refusal before the probe is logged, not silent.** `reachability
  probe (blank login): not sent, the lease ran out` - so a job that ends
  `timeout` on its second try says why there was no probe.
- **`writeLoginSilentResult(nullptr, …)` leaves `probeAnsweredAs` out**, as the
  record's `string?` null is serialised; the board never passes it (the tier is
  always the role's name or `refused`), and the writer's test holds both.
- **The one new line of `platformio.ini`** adds `agent/LoginSequence.cpp` to
  `[env:native]`'s `build_src_filter`; nothing of the bootstrap's flag block,
  pins or libraries moved (its report, meshmaster.dk `tasks/phase-4.2/reports/65.md`,
  was read first).
- **The display needs nothing**: `open_` is set for the length of the job, so
  line four reads `Job 2231 config.read` through the probe as through any wait.

## Not verified here (and why)

A session cannot put a repeater on the air, ask it a wrong password, or hear
what it answers a blank login right after one. The scripted repeater is the
whole evidence, here as in the .NET agent (A-4: "still not proved on the air").
Task p1.1-08's host harness is where this runs end to end against meshmaster.dk's
rotation once merged - its `HostMesh` answers a blank login as `handleLoginReq`
does and a wrong password with silence, and `tools/host-run.ps1`'s fourth click
is a rotation - and its resume rebases over this task's `ensureSession`.

1. **A rotation whose reply was lost, on the bench** (docs/console.md's walk,
   item 17). Through an `acl` pair with a read-only grant: Access tab → *Rotate
   on the repeater…*, and power the repeater off the moment the console prints
   `cli 'password <new>' try 1` so that the reply is lost and the row goes
   `unverified` (the job ends `unreachable` after the send). Power it back on.
   The next admin job (Read on the Config tab) must log `the claim carries the
   new candidate of an unverified credential: logging in afresh with it`,
   `login (admin) try 1: no reply (…)`, `reachability probe (blank login):
   answered at read-only (…)`, `the repeater is on the air: …`, `login (admin)
   try 2: no reply (…)`, `the repeater answered a blank login at read-only and
   then said nothing to the new candidate: …`; the serial console the three
   lines above; `jobs` **`unauthorized` after 3 transaction(s)**; the job's page
   `reason: silentAfterReachable`, `probeAnsweredAs: read-only`; and the Access
   tab must say the candidate was refused and the attempt after it must be
   handed the old candidate and log in (`login (admin) try 1: logged in as
   admin`), settling the row. If the `password` line *did* reach the repeater
   before the power went, the candidate is right, the first try logs in, and the
   row settles on the new one with no probe - also correct, and the log shows no
   probe line.
2. **The repeater off the air during the candidate job.** Same row, repeater
   powered off: `try 1: no reply`, `reachability probe (blank login): no reply`,
   `the blank login was not answered either: …`, `try 2: no reply`, `jobs`
   **`unreachable` after 3**, no data, and the Access tab unchanged (nothing is
   noted from a silence that taught nothing).
3. **The transaction count against `tx`.** `status` on the console must show
   `tx` moved by three for job 1 and job 2 - the probe is one packet, as every
   transaction is.
4. **The `refused` tier is unreachable on stock firmware**: no v1.17.1 repeater
   sends the legacy `LOGIN_FAIL`, so that path has the scripted repeater alone,
   as the .NET agent's `FakeCompanion.RefuseLogin` is alone there.
